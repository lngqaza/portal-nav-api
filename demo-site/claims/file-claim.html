We're here to help! Follow these simple steps to file your insurance claim.

Claim Filing Process

1

Report the Incident

Contact us immediately after an incident occurs. We're available 24/7.

2

Gather information about what happened and fill out our claim form.

3

Submit Documentation

Upload photos, receipts, and other supporting documents.

4

Claims Review

Our team reviews your claim and contacts you with next steps.

5

Settlement

Receive your settlement payment within 48 hours of approval.

6

Follow-Up

We'll check in to ensure you're satisfied with the resolution.

Claims FAQ

Q: How long does it take to process a claim?

A: Most claims are processed within 48 hours. Complex claims may take up to 2 weeks.

Q: What's the minimum claim amount?

A: There's no minimum. We handle all claim sizes.

Q: Can I file a claim online?

A: Yes! Use our online form above or call our 24/7 claims line.

Q: What if my claim is denied?

A: You can appeal the decision. We'll provide a detailed explanation and guidance on next steps.

FAQ

Common questions about your NovaSure policy

How do I submit a new claim?
Go to Claims → Submit New Claim. Select your claim type, fill in the incident details, upload supporting documents and click Submit. You'll receive a reference number by SMS immediately.
How long does a claim take to process?
Standard claims are assessed within 5–10 business days. Medical and disability claims may take up to 15 business days depending on the complexity and whether all documents have been submitted.
How do I update my banking details?
Go to Account → Banking Details. Changes take effect on the next payment cycle. You'll receive an SMS confirmation after saving.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address. The link expires after 30 minutes.
How do I add or change a beneficiary?
Go to My Policy → Beneficiaries. You can add, edit or remove beneficiaries at any time. Changes are effective immediately on submission.
How do I download my tax certificate?
Go to Statements. Your IT3b tax certificate for each tax year is available for download from 1 March following the end of that tax year.
What documents do I need for a retrenchment claim?
You will need: (1) A letter of retrenchment from your employer on company letterhead, (2) your latest payslip, (3) a copy of your ID. Upload these via Submit New Claim.
